Responsible for the monitoring and managing application security vulnerabilities
from penetration tests, **SAST**, **DAST**, and other sources, ensuring timely
resolution within SLAs.

#### Key Responsibilities

- Day-to-day monitoring of **SAST/SCA** tooling and remediation of open issues
within agreed severity SLAs
- Monthly monitoring of **DAST** scan reports
- Yearly penetration tests, supporting and remediation of found issues
- Developed reporting for key stakeholders (e.g. Engineering Leads, IT Sec Team,
 and Senior Management) to give a clear picture of our security posture at a
 given point in time
- Aided in the role out of Threat Modelling process as part of the SDLC.
 Responsible for signing off of the threat models created with the **STRIDE** methodology

#### Extra

- Part of the Laya Culture Team to roll out culture related initiatives
- Conducted PoC for role out of Github Copilot across the team, utilising Github
 Spaces, and the Github MCP server
- Part of the Engineering Knowledge sharing initiative, giving talks across the
 wider IT team on different topics, e.g. my AI rollout PoC, and good git hygiene
